Who We Are

MATE stands for Measurement, Assessment, Testing, and Evaluation.

MATE Education is an educational consultancy specialising in language assessment, educational measurement, curriculum alignment, and academic quality assurance.

We support universities, schools, and educational organisations in designing, developing, evaluating, and maintaining assessment systems.

Our work is grounded in the principle that effective assessment requires more than well-written test questions.

It depends on

  • Clearly defined constructs
  • Appropriate assessment tasks
  • Coherent test specifications
  • Defensible scoring procedures
  • Meaningful interpretation of results
  • Systematic quality assurance

We bring these components together to help institutions develop assessment practices that are technically sound, educationally meaningful, and sustainable.

Our Mission

To strengthen assessment and academic quality through specialist expertise, evidence-based practices, and sustainable institutional solutions.

Our Vision

To contribute to educational environments in which assessment is designed and used responsibly, supports meaningful educational decisions, and remains consistent and effective beyond changes in personnel or institutional circumstances.

Our Approach

Our Approach to Institutional Assessment Capacity

Assessment expertise often develops through years of professional experience, training, and institutional practice.

When experienced staff leave or change roles, institutions may lose not only individual expertise but also knowledge of established assessment procedures and standards.

MATE Education helps institutions address this challenge by developing documented assessment frameworks, shared procedures, quality assurance mechanisms, and continuing access to specialist expertise.

Our approach combines institutional collaboration with external professional support, allowing institutions to strengthen their internal practices while drawing on specialist knowledge when needed.

Our Experts

University-Based Expertise. Collaborative Solutions.

MATE Education brings together a multidisciplinary team of university academics and experienced specialists in language assessment, applied linguistics, educational measurement, psychometrics, and test development.

Our experts hold academic and teaching positions at universities and combine research experience with practical expertise in designing, implementing, and evaluating assessment systems.

The team includes language assessment specialists, experienced item writers, psychometricians, applied linguists, and educational measurement experts.

Rather than relying on a single consultant to address every aspect of assessment, we draw on complementary areas of expertise to form project teams according to institutional needs.

This collaborative model enables us to provide specialist support across

  • Assessment development
  • Psychometric analysis
  • Curriculum alignment
  • Scoring and standardisation
  • Quality assurance

Academic knowledge. Specialist expertise. Practical solutions.
