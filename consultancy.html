Institutional Projects & Consultancy

Specialist Support for Complex Assessment Challenges

Some institutional assessment needs extend beyond individual tests, established service packages, or routine quality assurance activities.

Areas of ConsultancyBespoke Institutional ProjectsCorporate Assessment Solutions

MATE Education works with universities, schools, educational organisations, and institutional partners to design and implement projects requiring specialist assessment expertise.

Our consultancy services are developed around the objectives, context, and resources of each institution.

Areas of Consultancy

Where we can support your institution

01

Assessment System Development

Designing or redesigning assessment frameworks, procedures, and quality assurance systems.

02

Test Validation and Evaluation

Examining assessment instruments and their intended uses through appropriate evidence, analyses, and evaluation procedures.

03

CEFR Alignment

Supporting the development and evaluation of assessment frameworks and instruments in relation to CEFR proficiency expectations.

04

Curriculum–Assessment Alignment

Reviewing the relationship between intended learning outcomes, instructional content, assessment tasks, and evidence of achievement.

05

Psychometric Analysis

Applying appropriate statistical and measurement methods to investigate item and test performance, score consistency, and assessment quality.

06

Programme Evaluation

Examining educational programmes through curriculum documentation, assessment evidence, implementation practices, and institutional objectives.

07

Institutional Assessment Capacity Building

Supporting the development of assessment policies, documentation, staff expertise, shared procedures, and quality assurance mechanisms.

Bespoke Institutional Projects

Project teams built around the expertise required

Projects may involve independent reviews, research and analysis, development of assessment frameworks and instruments, implementation support, professional learning, and ongoing evaluation.

We assemble specialist project teams according to the expertise required and work collaboratively with institutional stakeholders throughout the agreed project.

Our aim is to provide technically informed, context-sensitive solutions that institutions can implement and sustain.

Corporate Assessment Solutions

Assessing English for Professional Communication

Organisations need language assessment evidence that reflects the communication demands of professional contexts.

MATE Education supports the development of English language assessments aligned with defined workplace communication requirements.

Our services may include

  • Workplace language needs analysis
  • Assessment framework development
  • Test specifications
  • Workplace-relevant assessment tasks
  • Speaking and writing performance criteria
  • Scoring rubrics
  • Assessment reporting frameworks

Our approach begins by identifying the language abilities required in a particular professional context.

Assessment instruments are then designed to elicit relevant evidence of those abilities, with attention to task authenticity, assessment quality, and the intended use of results.

Corporate assessment projects are developed according to organisational needs and may be integrated with broader institutional consultancy services.
