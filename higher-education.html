Assessment Solutions for Higher Education

Specialist Assessment Support for Universities

University language assessment requires expertise across several interconnected areas, including test development, language proficiency, psychometrics, scoring, statistical analysis, and quality assurance.

Three Levels of SupportTest DevelopmentTesting Office PartnershipExternal Testing Office

Maintaining all these areas of expertise within a single institutional testing office can be challenging.

MATE Education provides universities with flexible access to specialist assessment services while allowing them to retain responsibility for their academic policies and decisions.

Our higher education solutions are organised around three levels of support.

01

Test Development

Assessment Instruments Designed for Your Institutional Context

We develop and review English language assessments aligned with institutional curricula, learning outcomes, and assessment purposes.

Our services cover

  • Placement tests
  • Progress tests
  • Achievement tests
  • Proficiency tests

Depending on the project, development services may include

  • Construct definition
  • Test specifications and blueprints
  • Item writing and review
  • Reading and listening task development
  • Writing and speaking assessment tasks
  • Rating scales and scoring guidelines
  • Moderation and quality control
  • Assessment documentation
  • Pilot testing and statistical analysis, where appropriate

Our approach considers the linguistic and cognitive demands of assessment tasks, the quality of the evidence they elicit, fairness, and the intended interpretation and use of test scores.

Test development services can be commissioned for individual assessment instruments or broader assessment programmes.

02

Testing Office Partnership

Strengthening Your Existing Assessment Team

The MATE Testing Office Partnership is designed for universities that already have an internal testing team but require additional specialist expertise or ongoing technical support.

We work collaboratively with institutional staff to strengthen assessment development procedures, improve scoring consistency, support data analysis, and maintain quality standards.

Depending on institutional priorities, services may include

  • Review and development of test specifications
  • Specialist item writing and item review
  • Assessment task development
  • Rating scale development and revision
  • Rater training and standardisation
  • Moderation procedures
  • Standard setting
  • Item and test analysis
  • Technical reporting
  • Assessment quality assurance
  • Documentation and staff development

The university’s testing office continues to manage its assessment activities, while MATE provides specialist support in agreed areas.

Partnerships may be established for a defined project or maintained throughout the academic year.

03

External Testing Office

Your Institution. Our Assessment Expertise.

The MATE External Testing Office provides an alternative model for institutions seeking comprehensive assessment development and quality assurance services without maintaining a full multidisciplinary testing team internally.

Under this model, MATE assumes responsibility for agreed assessment development and technical quality assurance functions.

Our university-based specialists work as an external assessment team, providing expertise in language assessment, item development, psychometrics, CEFR alignment, statistical analysis, and assessment quality assurance.

Depending on the institutional agreement, services may include

  • Annual assessment development planning
  • Development of placement, progress, achievement, and proficiency tests
  • Test specification development and maintenance
  • Item writing, review, and revision
  • Item-bank development and maintenance
  • Assessment moderation and scoring support
  • Statistical analyses and technical reporting
  • Regular consultation and review meetings
  • Ongoing assessment quality assurance
  • Periodic evaluation of assessment procedures

A designated institutional coordinator serves as the primary point of contact between the university and MATE Education.

The institution retains ownership of its academic policies, assessment decisions, and student-related responsibilities. MATE provides the agreed specialist assessment services and technical support.

A Flexible Institutional Model

The External Testing Office can be structured as an annual service agreement, with the scope of assessment activities, reporting arrangements, and consultation procedures determined jointly.

This model gives institutions access to multidisciplinary assessment expertise while supporting continuity in assessment development and quality assurance.
