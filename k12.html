Assessment Solutions for K–12

Better Assessment. Better Evidence of Learning.

Effective assessment should do more than produce grades or test scores.

School Assessment ReviewAssessment System DesignCEFR BenchmarkProgress & Parent ReportingMaarif Modeli Support

It should provide meaningful evidence of student learning, identify areas for development, inform instructional decisions, and support academic improvement.

MATE Education works with schools and school groups to develop assessment practices aligned with their curricula, student populations, and educational objectives.

Our services support schools in strengthening the relationship between intended learning outcomes, classroom instruction, assessment evidence, and academic monitoring.

01

School Assessment Review

Evaluating Assessment Practices Across the School

The School Assessment Review examines existing assessment frameworks, instruments, scoring procedures, curriculum alignment, and the use of assessment evidence.

Depending on the scope, we review assessment documentation, sample tasks and tests, performance criteria, reporting practices, and available evidence of student achievement.

We examine how consistently assessment principles are applied across teachers, departments, grade levels, or campuses.

The review results in a report identifying strengths, development priorities, and recommended actions.

02

School Assessment System Design

Building Coherent Assessment Practices

We support schools in developing assessment frameworks and procedures that provide consistent and meaningful evidence of learning.

Services may include

  • Assessment policies and frameworks
  • Curriculum-aligned assessment planning
  • Common assessment instruments
  • Performance-based assessment tasks
  • Assessment criteria and scoring rubrics
  • Moderation and standardisation procedures
  • Student progress monitoring
  • Assessment reporting frameworks
  • Quality assurance processes

Our approach considers the age, learning needs, curriculum, and assessment purposes relevant to each school context.

For school groups operating across multiple campuses, we can support the development of shared assessment standards and procedures while allowing for appropriate contextual differences.

03

CEFR Benchmark Assessment

Evidence of English Language Development

MATE supports the development of curriculum-independent English language benchmark assessments informed by the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages (CEFR).

These assessments are designed to provide evidence of learners’ English language performance in relation to defined proficiency expectations.

Assessment development considers communicative language activities, task characteristics, linguistic demands, and performance expectations.

Appropriate development, review, and validation procedures are used to support claims about CEFR alignment.

Benchmark assessment results can contribute to institutional decisions about language learning progress and areas requiring further support.

04

Student Progress and Parent Reporting

Making Assessment Results Meaningful

Assessment information is most useful when it can be interpreted and acted upon.

We help schools develop reporting frameworks that communicate student performance and progress clearly to teachers, school leaders, students, and parents.

Reports may include

  • Performance across assessed skills
  • Identified strengths
  • Areas requiring further development
  • Progress over time
  • Descriptive performance information
  • Recommendations for subsequent learning support

Our emphasis is on communicating meaningful evidence of learning rather than presenting numerical scores without interpretation.

05

Türkiye Yüzyılı Maarif Modeli Implementation Support

From Curriculum Alignment to Classroom Implementation

Implementing a curriculum framework requires more than revising instructional plans or assessment documents.

It involves establishing meaningful connections between curriculum expectations, learning outcomes, learning experiences, assessment evidence, feedback, and academic monitoring.

MATE Education supports schools in reviewing and strengthening their implementation of the Türkiye Yüzyılı Maarif Modeli.

Our services focus on the educational and assessment-related aspects of implementation.

Areas of Support

Curriculum Alignment

Examining the relationship between curriculum expectations, learning outcomes, instructional planning, and assessment practices.

Evidence of Learning

Reviewing how classroom activities, assessment tasks, and student work provide evidence of the intended knowledge and skills.

Formative Assessment

Strengthening approaches to monitoring learning, identifying learning needs, providing feedback, and informing subsequent instruction.

Performance-Based Assessment

Supporting the development of meaningful performance tasks, assessment criteria, scoring rubrics, and feedback procedures.

Skills-Based Assessment

Examining how assessment practices reflect the relevant skills and learning expectations identified in the curriculum framework.

Implementation Review and Academic Monitoring

Identifying areas of alignment, inconsistencies, and development priorities across classrooms, departments, and school levels.

Our Implementation Approach

We begin with a structured review of curriculum documents, instructional plans, assessment instruments, and relevant evidence of classroom implementation.

Findings inform an implementation roadmap outlining what should be maintained, strengthened, redesigned, developed, and monitored.

MaintainStrengthenRedesignDevelopMonitor

Institutions may then continue with targeted development activities, professional learning, or ongoing academic quality support.
