MATE Professional Learning

Develop Specialist Expertise. Strengthen Professional Practice.

Certificate Programmes, Specialist Courses & Workshops

OverviewCertificate ProgrammesCourses & WorkshopsBespoke TrainingLearning FormatsEvents

MATE Education offers research-informed, practice-oriented professional development programmes that help educators and assessment professionals translate theoretical knowledge into effective professional practice.

Our programmes are designed for

English language teachersUniversity instructorsTesting office staffItem writersAssessment specialistsTeacher trainersCurriculum developersProgramme coordinatorsAcademic leaders

working in higher education, K–12 schools, and other educational institutions.

Rather than focusing exclusively on theoretical concepts, our programmes engage participants in analysing authentic educational challenges, developing and evaluating assessment materials, interpreting evidence, and making informed professional decisions.

Participants develop practical approaches, tools, and resources they can apply directly in their professional contexts.

Programmes are delivered online or face-to-face, with opportunities for individual enrolment and institution-specific professional development.

01

Certificate Programmes

Structured Professional Development

Our certificate programmes provide opportunities for sustained professional learning in selected areas of language assessment, test development, curriculum design, and educational quality.

Programmes are organised around defined learning outcomes and may include guided learning activities, practical assignments, assessment tasks, and professional portfolios.

Programme content, duration, assessment requirements, and certification conditions are specified for each course.

Certificate

Certificate in Language Assessment Practice

A practice-oriented programme for teachers, testing office staff, and assessment professionals involved in developing and evaluating language assessments.

The programme focuses on connecting key principles of language assessment with the practical decisions involved in assessment design, development, scoring, and evaluation.

Topics may include

  • Assessment purposes and construct definition
  • Test specifications
  • Item writing and review
  • Assessing receptive and productive skills
  • Rating scales and scoring
  • Standardisation and moderation
  • Item analysis and assessment quality

Participants apply their learning to authentic assessment development and evaluation tasks.

Programme availability, duration, and certification requirements will be announced separately.

02

Specialist Courses & Workshops

Focused Learning. Practical Application.

Our specialist courses and workshops address specific areas of professional practice and provide opportunities for focused development.

Assessment Literacy

Assessment purposes, validity, reliability, fairness, assessment quality, and the interpretation and use of assessment evidence.

Item Writing and Test Development

Test specifications, item-writing principles, distractor development, item review, and quality assurance.

CEFR and Language Assessment

CEFR descriptors, proficiency expectations, assessment design, and alignment.

Assessing Reading and Listening

Construct definition, cognitive processes, task design, item development, and assessment quality.

Assessing Writing and Speaking

Task design, assessment criteria, rating scales, rater judgement, scoring consistency, and standardisation.

Assessment for Learning and Feedback

Formative assessment, evidence of learning, feedback practices, and instructional decision-making.

Standard Setting

Performance standards, cut-score decisions, standard-setting procedures, and defensible score interpretation.

Assessment Data and Item Analysis

Item facility, discrimination, distractor analysis, reliability, and the interpretation of assessment statistics.

AI in Assessment

Responsible AI-assisted assessment development, construct representation, item quality, fairness, and human oversight.

03

Bespoke Professional Development

Training Designed Around Institutional Needs

Institutions differ in their assessment practices, professional development priorities, and available expertise.

MATE Education designs institution-specific professional development programmes based on the needs of participating staff and the institution’s educational objectives.

Programmes may include targeted workshops, extended training series, collaborative development projects, and follow-up activities.

Where appropriate, professional development can be integrated with broader assessment system development or quality assurance projects.
