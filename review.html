Understand Where You Are. Decide Where to Go Next.

Overview

How the parts of your assessment system work together

The MATE Assessment System Review provides a structured evaluation of an institution’s existing assessment system, examining the relationship between assessment design, implementation, scoring, reporting, and quality assurance.

The purpose is not simply to identify weaknesses in individual tests, but to understand how effectively the different components of the assessment system work together.

What We Review

Seven areas of the assessment system

Depending on the agreed scope, the review may examine:

01

Assessment Frameworks and Governance

Assessment policies, institutional standards, responsibilities, and decision-making procedures.

02

Curriculum and Construct Alignment

The relationship between curriculum objectives, intended constructs, assessment tasks, and learning outcomes.

03

Test Specifications and Assessment Instruments

Test blueprints, specifications, task characteristics, item quality, and construct representation.

04

Assessment Development Procedures

Item writing, item review, moderation, documentation, and quality control.

05

Scoring and Standardisation

Assessment criteria, rating scales, scoring procedures, rater training, and standardisation practices.

06

Assessment Data and Statistical Evidence

Available item statistics, test reliability evidence, score distributions, and the interpretation and use of assessment results.

07

Quality Assurance and Institutional Capacity

Existing review procedures, documentation, staff expertise, and mechanisms for maintaining assessment standards.

What You Receive

Assessment Development Roadmap

Following the review, we provide a structured report identifying

  • Existing strengths and established practices
  • Areas requiring further development
  • Potential risks to assessment quality
  • Gaps in documentation or procedures
  • Recommended actions and priorities
  • Opportunities for longer-term improvement

Recommendations are organised around what should be maintained, strengthened, redesigned, or developed.

MaintainStrengthenRedesignDevelop

The roadmap provides a practical basis for institutional planning and subsequent assessment development.

What Happens Next?

Continue on your own, or with us.

Institutions may choose to implement the recommendations independently or continue working with MATE Education.

We offer targeted assessment development, specialist consultancy, and ongoing institutional partnerships.

Where institutional needs extend beyond our established services, we develop a bespoke solution reflecting the institution’s context, priorities, and resources.

No two institutions need exactly the same assessment system. Neither should they receive exactly the same solution.
